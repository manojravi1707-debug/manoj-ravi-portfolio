Engineering Story & Background

I completed my Bachelor of Engineering in Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) at the prestigious Sri Shakthi Institute of Engineering and Technology (2020–2024), graduating with a solid academic GPA of 7.8 / 10.

From the onset of my engineering education, I was drawn to the tangible bridge between physical electron circuits and intelligent computing algorithms. My engineering coursework and labs exposed me to microcontrollers, communication protocols, signal processing, and low-level firmware.

Recognizing the transformative value of software in modern engineering, I expanded my skills into Python, C programming, MySQL, and Linux. Furthermore, I delved into Robotic Process Automation (RPA) with UiPath Studio, mastering the industry-standard Robotic Enterprise (RE) Framework to automate repetitive data extraction and business processes.

In corporate operations at Hexaware Technologies, I honed exceptional communication skills, active listening, and problem resolution under stringent service level agreements (SLAs), managing diverse customer interactions daily with dedication and empathy.
